The inspiration journal

Ideas for your kind of home.

Easy ways to explore color, texture, lighting and decor with your own personal style.

01. Start with a palette

Choose two or three complementary colors. Cream, earthy brown and muted green can create a relaxed, coordinated look.

02. Mix natural textures

Combine wood, cotton, linen and woven details to bring depth and warmth into a room.

03. Layer your lighting

Use general lighting alongside reading lamps and softer accent lights for different moods.

04. Style with intention

Choose a few meaningful objects, add a plant or artwork, and leave enough breathing room.
